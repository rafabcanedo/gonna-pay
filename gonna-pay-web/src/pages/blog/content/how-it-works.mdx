Splitting costs with friends shouldn't require a spreadsheet. Gonna Pay handles the math — you just log what you spent.

## 1. Add your contacts

Start by adding the people you split costs with. Organize them by category — **Family**, **Friends**, or **Work** — so you always know who you're dealing with.

## 2. Create a group

Got a trip coming up? A shared apartment? A weekly dinner? Create a group and add the relevant contacts to it. Groups give context to every expense you log.

## 3. Log a cost

Add an expense to a group and set the total. Gonna Pay automatically splits it equally among all members — or you can set custom percentages if someone paid more.

## 4. Track who owes what

Every cost generates splits. You can see at a glance how much each person owes and who has already settled up.

## 5. Check your dashboard

The dashboard gives you a full overview: your recent expenses, spending by category, and a breakdown of your current month. No surprises.

---

That's it. No complicated setup, no hidden steps. Create a group, log a cost, split it. Gonna Pay figures out the rest.

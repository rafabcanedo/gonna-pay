Gonna Pay is still growing. Here's a closer look at what's coming next.

## Real contacts

Right now, when you add a contact, you fill in a name, email, and phone number and that's it. The contact lives only in your account. It could be a real person or a placeholder you typed just to get a split going.

That's about to change.

With real contacts, you'll search for someone by name or email directly in Gonna Pay. If they have an account, you'll find them. You add them as a contact, and from that point on, they're linked to their actual account. You can still customize how their name appears to you, but the connection underneath is real.

This matters because it's the foundation for everything that comes after: notifications, a shared view of costs, and a more honest picture of who owes what to whom.

## Wallet

The Wallet will give you a single, consolidated view of your financial standing across everything in Gonna Pay: every group, every contact, every split.

Today, figuring out the full picture means jumping between costs, checking splits one by one, and doing the math yourself. The Wallet removes all of that. You'll see at a glance what you're owed, what you owe, and where each balance comes from.

## Reminders

The dashboard already has a Reminders widget. You can set a name, a value, and a date. It works, but only for as long as your session lasts. Refresh the page and everything is gone. There's also no connection to your actual costs or groups.

We're making reminders real. They'll be saved to your account so they persist across sessions, and you'll be able to link a reminder to a specific cost or group. If you need to remember to settle a dinner or pay someone back for a trip, it stays there until you take care of it.

## Notifications

Splits only work when everyone stays informed. Right now, there's no way to know when someone adds a cost that includes you. You'd have to check manually or wait for someone to tell you.

We're adding a notification system so that when a cost that involves you is logged, you'll know right away. No more chasing people for updates or finding out about a split after the fact.

## Split settlement

When a cost is linked to a group and you cover less than 100%, Gonna Pay automatically generates splits for the remaining amount. That part works well. What doesn't exist yet is a way to close them.

Once a split is created, it just sits there with no status. There's no way to say it's been paid. We're building a settlement flow so you can mark a split as settled, both sides see the update, the balance clears, and you have a record that it's done.

## More categories, with a new design

The current categories are Dinner, Lunch, Travel, Entertainment, and Others. They cover the basics, but they don't cover everything. We're adding more to better match how people actually spend.

Along with the new categories, we're rethinking how they look and feel across the app. The goal is something cleaner and easier to scan, especially when you have a lot of costs across different contexts.

These are the features we're most focused on right now. Some are already in progress, others are close behind. Stay tuned.

## How can I start?
There isn't a right way to start — you can use this app to manage your costs on your own, or especially for shared costs with friends, family, and coworkers. There is also a simple documentation explaining how things work and the concepts of each page available throughout the app.

## What is GonnaPay
GonnaPay is an application developed for you to manage your finances for a specific event or trip, by yourself or with friends and family.
Here you will find Costs, Groups, Contacts, and other things, but the focus is especially on the things mentioned in this section.
In the docs, you will learn what each feature is in this application and how to interact with them.

## Create your account
The first step, of course, is to create an account to start using the app.

You can create your account on the [Register](/signup) page.

After that, you can sign in on the [Login](/signin) page and start using GonnaPay.

## Create your first contact
We recommend starting by creating a contact, as this is an important step to create groups and share costs with others.

You can create a contact on the [My Contacts](/my-contacts) page by providing their name, email, phone, and a category (Work, Family, or Friend).

After that, you can share costs with your contacts and groups.

**Important note**
You can only create a cost associated with a group if you have already created a contact, created a group, and added that contact to the group.

## Create your first cost
As we mentioned, costs are an important theme in this application — maybe the core of it. There are two ways to create a cost: a solo cost or a cost with an existing group, which determines what percentage you would like to pay. We'll explain this in more detail later. In solo costs, you cannot choose what percentage to pay — you always pay 100%.

## Create your first group
For groups, you can create a group with the contacts you've already added. First, create a group on the [Groups](/groups) page, and then access the group details to add a member (a contact you've already added).

## Payments
Payments gives you a global view of your costs. You will find solo payments — which can be recurring or one-time — and shared payments with other people and groups. There is also a chart with filters to help you visualize your spending over time.

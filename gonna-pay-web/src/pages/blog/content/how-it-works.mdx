Gonna Pay is built around three concepts: costs, groups, and contacts. Once you understand how they connect, everything else follows.

## Costs

A cost is anything you spent — dinner, gas, an Airbnb, a round of drinks. Log it, set the amount, and Gonna Pay takes care of the rest.

When creating a cost, you can link it to a group and set the percentage you want to cover. Both are optional — if you skip the group, it becomes a solo cost and you cover 100% of it.

Each cost has a category: **Dinner**, **Lunch**, **Entertainment**, **Travel**, or **Others**.

## Cost splits

When a cost is linked to a group and you cover less than 100%, the remaining amount is automatically split among the group members. You never create splits manually — they're generated by the cost itself.

To have splits, you need three things: a contact, a group with that contact as a member, and a cost linked to that group with a percentage below 100%.

## Groups

A group is a shared context for expenses. Create one for a trip, a shared apartment, or a regular dinner — add the contacts that belong to it, and reuse it for every cost that fits.

Like costs, each group has a category: **Dinner**, **Lunch**, **Entertainment**, **Travel**, or **Others**.

## Payments

Payments gives you a high-level view of your finances — solo costs and shared costs in one place, so you always know where your money is going.

Gonna Pay is still growing. Here's a preview of what we're working on next.

## Wallet

The Wallet is the next big piece. It will give you a consolidated view of everything you're owed and everything you owe — across all groups and contacts, in one place.

No more digging through individual costs to figure out where you stand.

## Advanced reports

The dashboard today shows your spending by category and by period. We're expanding that with deeper analytics: trends over time, comparisons between months, and a breakdown by group or contact.

## Notifications

When someone logs a cost that includes you, you'll know. We're adding a notification system to keep everyone in the loop without requiring manual follow-ups.

## More cost categories

Dinner, Lunch, Travel, Entertainment, Others — the current set covers the basics. We're adding more categories to better reflect how people actually spend.

# How can I start?

There isn't a right way to start — you can use this app to manage your costs on your own, or especially for shared costs with friends, family, and coworkers.

## Create your account

The first step is to create an account to start using the app.

You can create your account on the [Register](/signup) page. After that, sign in on the [Login](/signin) page and you're ready to go.

## Create your first contact

We recommend starting by creating a contact, as this is an important step to create groups and share costs with others.

You can create a contact on the [My Contacts](/my-contacts) page by providing their name, email, phone, and a category (Work, Family, or Friend).

## Create your first group

Once you have a contact, you can create a group on the [Groups](/groups) page. After creating it, open the group details to add your contact as a member.

## Create your first cost

With a group set up, you can create a cost on the [Costs](/costs) page. You can link it to a group and set the percentage you'd like to cover — or keep it as a solo cost with no group attached.

<Callout type="important">To create a cost associated with a group, you need to have a contact, a group, and that contact added as a member of the group.</Callout>

## Payments

Payments gives you a global view of your costs. You will find solo payments and shared payments with other people and groups. There is also a chart with filters to help you visualize your spending over time.

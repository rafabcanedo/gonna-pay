# How it works

This doc explains how each section of this application works.

## Costs

A cost is a specific payment at a specific moment. When you're travelling, going out for dinner, or doing something else, you can log a cost for it.

When creating a cost, you can optionally link it to a group and set the percentage you'd like to cover.

<Callout type="tip">Group and percentage are optional. If you don't add a group, it becomes a solo cost — you cover 100% of it.</Callout>

Each cost has a category:

<CategoryBadges items={["Dinner", "Lunch", "Entertainment", "Travel", "Others"]} />

## Cost Splits

When a cost is linked to a group and you cover less than 100%, the remaining amount is automatically split among the group members. You don't create splits manually — they appear as a result of the cost.

<Callout type="important">To have splits, you need a contact, a group with that contact as a member, and a cost linked to that group with a percentage below 100%.</Callout>

## Groups

Groups are a way to keep your costs organized. You can create a group and add your contacts to it. This allows you to associate costs with a group, and if you want to use the same group for another cost, you can.

Each group also has a category that represents the type of expense it covers:

<CategoryBadges items={["Dinner", "Lunch", "Entertainment", "Travel", "Others"]} />

## Payments

Payments gives you a high-level view of your finances, containing solo costs and shared costs.
